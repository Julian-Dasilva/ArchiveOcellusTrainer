# Scenarios and their oracles

This page lists, for each mechanic, the Vitest scenario and the browser scenario that prove it, the input each one sends, and where its expected value comes from. Use it before moving code between owners. A mechanic whose rows lose a scenario, or whose oracle becomes a value read from the code under test, has lost its safety net. Change this page in the same pull request that changes a scenario.

## Input kinds

- `click`: a real `mousedown` on the trainer's canvas at the projection of the tile, `Viewport.projectTile`. Vitest mounts the SDK's canvas viewport in place of the 3D one, because jsdom has no WebGL (`test/setupFiles.ts`), so the projection is that viewport's. `drill.click(tile, { ctrlKey })` in `src/content/ocellus/tests/drill.ts` sends it, and page tests send the same click with `clickTile`. The SDK's ClickController resolves the tile, queues the order through InputController, and the next `world.tickWorld()` hands it to `Player.moveTo`. The fight log's `walk-ordered` input proves which tile the click resolved to. A click on `UNDER_BOSS` while the boss is drawn attacks him instead, and his `player-hit-judged` events show each attack's launch, landing and requested damage. A click on a void rift's tile attacks the rift. The drill's canvas is 800 by 800 pixels, 12 tiles each way around the player, so a click reaches any tile up to 5 rows from the player.
- `press`: the same real `mousedown` as `click`, sent by `drill.clickRightBeforeTick(tile)`, which calls `world.tickWorld()` right after it with no other task between, as a click in the frame before a tick is in the browser.
- `page`: a real DOM click or key press on the mounted page through `loadPage` in `page.tsx`, or through `loadFullFight` in `FullFightChrome.test.tsx`.
- `order`: a direct `Player.moveTo` through `drill.walkTo` or `drill.player().moveTo`. It skips ClickController, the input queue and the Control-key mapping.
- `none`: no player input. The drill's options and real World ticks drive the run.
- Browser: `scripts/verify.sh browser` dispatches an untimed `click_tile` with no modifier, a timed `click_tile`, `click_after_tick`, a `tile_pixel` click, and the frame clock's `click` (including the two clicks of `drive_click_correction`) as `MouseEvent`s on `#world` inside the page, on the animation frame at their time. Each one hit-tests with `document.elementFromPoint` and does not dispatch unless that element is `#world`. The corner nub passes `walk`, and only that event holds Shift. Every other projected tile click is plain. An untimed Control-click goes through CDP's Input domain. Every browser scenario runs on the full fight's Phase 4, in 3D, the trainer's only view. Every floor and rift click aims at the SDK's 3D projection of a platform-local tile, read through the probe's `tileCentre`, and `projectedClicksOrderTheirTiles` checks that every `click_tile` click on the walkway, back wall, corner, pathing and holes pages ordered its tile. A boss click is `click_boss`: on the first frame of its tick it right-clicks up from the projection of (5, 5) until the SDK's menu offers Attack first, and the left click goes at that height in the same frame. Side-panel clicks (tabs, inventory slots, the special orb and the Keybinds tab buttons) go through `click_panel` and `agent-browser mouse`, and prayers through `click_after_tick` at `panel_offset`, at points measured on a 1280x800 canvas and kept at their distance from the canvas's bottom right, or top right for the orbs. The cue pages' first click on the canvas centre is the only offset click left.

Every Vitest scenario built on `startDrill` ticks the real world through GET READY, so the first world tick after `startDrill` or `drill.restart` is P4 tick 0.

Vitest cannot draw the 3D view offline, because cache models and spotanims do not load. `drill.drawnHoles()` and `drill.drawnMarks()` read what the 3D view is given instead: the tiles of the region's floor holes and explosion marks that are not destroyed and are visible, which is how `Viewport3d` keeps and draws an entity.

## Oracle sources

- `data`: a value in `src/content/ocellus/js/OcellusTimings.ts`, named by its `OCELLUS_P4` key. Each value there carries its own footage, wiki, cache or owner source.
- `wiki`: an Old School RuneScape Wiki page. The data file cites the pages its values come from.
- `hand`: worked out by hand from `data` or `wiki` values, with the working given here.
- `seeded`: the output of a seeded roll, pinned from a passing run. Only its bounds are independent, and they come from `data`.
- `rolls`: a player's attack roll worked out from the seed alone. The combat stream is mulberry32 seeded with the seed XOR `0x5eed0c0b`, drawn once per SDK `Random.get()` in the player's attack step. Each hit draws accuracy first and, when the draw is at most the hit chance, draws damage as ⌊draw × (max + 1)⌋. A Twisted Bow shot on Ocellus has hit chance 44 490 / 162 653 and max hit 62, from the `wiki` ranged formulas with Ranged 99, the ranged setup's +233 attack and +107 strength, and the bow's passive at Ocellus's Magic capped to 250. A scythe swing on Jab hits three times with hit chance 19 960 / 48 693 and maxes 35, 17 and 8, from Attack and Strength 99, the melee setup's +118 crush and +139 strength and Inquisitor's 2.5%. On seed 100 the first shot requests 54 and the first swing 31, 7 and 5. On seed 1 the first six shots request 0, 0, 0, 0, 12 and 33, over 9 draws with the seventh. The same working gives the pinned seed 4 values 8, 44 and 62, and 4, 12 and 8. An arrow from a tile 3 tiles from the boss lands 3 ticks after it is fired, and from 1 or 2 tiles, 2 ticks: the SDK's hit delay ⌊(3 + distance) / 6⌋ + 1 plus the tick the projectile is queued.

## Tests that bypass real input or real ticks

Each stays until the slice that replaces its API, which then moves the claim onto real input and deletes the bypass. An entry that names no slice belongs to a mechanic the approved migration does not move, and it keeps its bypass until a slice is scoped for it. A scan of the TypeScript syntax tree of every `*.test.ts` and `*.test.tsx` file in `src/content/ocellus/tests/` produced the counts and lines below.
