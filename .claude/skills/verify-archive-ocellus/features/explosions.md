# Personal explosions

Five ticks after each lifeforce hit, Ocellus marks the tile the player starts that tick on, and marks again every 2 ticks, 6 times by default. Each mark plays the game's own effect, cache spotanim 4339, on the marked tile: on the mark tick long thin purple shards stand high around the player's head and close in on the tile, then a purple glow fills it, and a tick later grey dust and spikes burst out there. A player who starts that tick on the marked tile takes 16. The mark captures the tile before the player moves, so a player who moves during the mark tick leaves it behind, and a step first taken on the impact tick is too late. Standing under the boss gives no cover. Two sounds set the rhythm players step to: synth 12638 starts each series two ticks after the lifeforce hit, and synth 12537 plays on every mark.

## Sub-features

- `explosion-series-start` logs `explosion-series-started` at P4 tick 14 (inner void) and 45 (outer void), two ticks after each lifeforce hit, and plays 12638 on the player's tile.
- `explosion-mark` logs `explosion-marked` at P4 tick 17, 19, 21, 23, 25 and 27 (inner void) and 48 to 58 (outer void), with the tile the player started that tick on, and places the mark on that tile and plays 12537 on the player's tile.
- `explosion-impact` logs `explosion-resolved` a tick after each mark. The outcome is `hit` with a 16 receipt when the player starts the impact tick on the marked tile, `dodged` when the player starts it 1 tile away, and `avoided` when 2 or more tiles away. The mark bursts either way.
- `explosion-dodge` cuts the player's attack delay by 3, never below 0, on each `dodged` impact, and records the delay before and after in the outcome's `attackDelay`. A Scythe or a Twisted bow on Rapid then attacks every 2 ticks while each mark is dodged; the full fight's blowpipe on Rapid already attacks every 2 ticks, and keeps doing so through the dodges. A hit or a 2-tile run leaves the delay alone.
- `explosion-under-boss` records `underBoss: true` when the marked tile is under the boss. Such a mark still hits.
- `explosion-damage-reduction` cuts each player hit that lands on the boss while the explosions occur, from the first mark to the last impact (17 to 28 and 48 to 59), by 60% of the attacker's roll, rounded down, then caps it to the boss's HP as the SDK caps any hit, and logs it as `player-hit-judged` with outcome `reduced`. A Division glaive the reduction cuts to 0 drains nothing, so the second glaive rolls against the Defence the first one actually leaves. A later attack counts only glaives still in flight, so once both glaives land it rolls against the Defence they left. A hit the boss blocks still lands as 0.
- `explosion-run-end` removes a mark still waiting for its impact when the run ends. A kill on an impact tick ends the run before that impact resolves, and the mark no longer draws under the result panel.
- `explosion-choices` select the series with the `explosions` choice: `v2-tell-then-six` (default, marks from hit + 5, 17 to 27, in step with sound 12537), or `wiki-six` (marks from hit + 3, 15 to 25), and the reduction with `explosionDamageReduction`: `wiki-60` (default, the Strategies page) or `wiki-50` (the Ocellus page). The page uses each default; Vitest drives the others.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `http://127.0.0.1:8002/` and stand still after the lifeforce hit at P4 tick 12. The mark appears under the player at 17, glows halfway through the tick, and a 16 lands at 18.
- Click a neighbouring tile on each mark tick, or keep walking, and every mark is left behind.
- Click the boss, then on each mark tick click one tile away and click the boss again. The blowpipe keeps firing every 2 ticks. A click 2 tiles away runs, so it avoids the mark without the speed-up. Each hit that lands from 17 to 28 takes 60% less off the boss bar.
- Listen for 12638 and 12537. **Sound effects** and **Area sounds** in the side panel's Sound section start ticked until the player unticks one.

## Driving it with agent-browser

Preconditions:

- The baseline preconditions in the index hold, with the default ranged setup.
- Every page runs the whole cycle, so the drain, the inner void and the transition attacks also act. The recipes below use `practice=1`, so no other hit ends the run first.
- A step on a mark tick is `step_on_marks` in `scripts/verify.sh`: on the first frame of the tick before each mark, it reads the player's tile, adds the step along the row, and clicks that tile's `tileCentre` with a `MouseEvent` on `#world`, sent only when `document.elementFromPoint` is `#world`. It turns back on each later mark.

- **Sound on.** Open `?seed=75&practice=1` (`ORB_QUERY`) and turn on the SDK's sound and area sound the way a returning player's saved settings would: read the JSON in `localStorage` key `osrs-sdk:settings` (`{"version":1,"values":{...}}`), set `values.playsAudio` and `values.playsAreaAudio` to `true`, and write it back with `agent-browser eval`. Open the same link again, which reads it. Then wrap `AudioBufferSourceNode.prototype.start` with `agent-browser eval` so each start records the P4 tick, how far into that tick it came on the page's animation clock (`document.timeline`, from the frame that started the tick), the buffer's length and sample rate, and the audio context's state.
- **Dodge on mark ticks.** On the tick before mark 17, step `DODGE_STEP_TILES` (2) tiles along the row. Copy the canvas to `explosion-left.png` 85% into tick 17, inside the animation frame that reaches it. It shows the purple glow on (5, 1), off the canvas centre, and the player two tiles away. Step back and forth the same way before marks 19, 21, 23, 25 and 27. `log()` shows a `walk-ordered` input in rows 17, 19, 21, 23, 25 and 27, the first mark on (5, 1), and `avoided` at 18, 20, 22, 24, 26 and 28.
- **Outside the series.** Copy the canvas to `explosion-before.png` 25% into P4 tick 15, before the first mark, and to `explosion-after.png` 25% into tick 29, after the inner series. Neither shows shards over the player's head.
- **Late step.** Arm a canvas copy to `explosion-standing.png` 25% into tick 48, then step two tiles on the first frame of tick 48. The step takes effect on tick 49, the impact tick. The copy shows the spotanim's shards standing high around the head of the player, who has not moved yet. `log()` shows the mark at 48 on the tile the player stood on, no input in row 48, the step in row 49, and `explosion-resolved` at 49 as a hit on that tile with receipt `EXPLOSION_EXPECTED.lateHit`, `{requested 14, applied 14, beforeHp 62, afterHp 48, prayer null}`, while row 49 ends with the player elsewhere. The player has 62, not 99, because the rest of the cycle has hit them by then.
- **Hear the sounds.** Wait for tick 59. The recorded starts at 22050 Hz match `EXPLOSION_SOUNDS`: those with 14332 samples (synth 12638) fall at ticks 14 and 45, and those with 8820 samples (synth 12537) at 17 to 27 and 48 to 58, every 2 ticks. Each runs on a `running` context. Other starts, such as the player's hit sound, have another rate. Set both sound settings back to `false` before the next page.
- **Camp under the boss.** Open `?seed=1&practice=1` (`DRILL_QUERY`). On P4 tick 1, while the boss is hidden, click `UNDER_BOSS_TILE` (5, 5), so the click walks the player there instead of attacking. Copy the canvas to `explosion-under.png` 85% into tick 17. It shows the player's tile outline inside the boss's footprint and the purple glow on the inner void at the canvas centre. Wait for tick 29. `log()` shows every inner `explosion-resolved` at 18 to 28 on (5, 5) with `underBoss: true` and a hit, leaving the HP in `EXPLOSION_EXPECTED.under`: 61, 26, 0, 75, 42 and 6. The inner void floor hits the camping player too, and Practice restores full HP after the hit that leaves 0 at 22.
- **Speed through the dodges.** Open `?seed=1&practice=1`. On P4 tick 12 (`DODGE_BOSS_CLICK_TICK`), click the hovering boss with `click_boss`. For each inner mark, step one row on the tick before it, south first and then back, and click the boss again on the mark tick at the height `click_boss` found. Wait for tick 34 (`DODGE_SPEEDUP_LAST_TICK`). `log()` shows `dodged` at 18 to 28, and the blowpipe's `combatDraws` on `DODGE_SPEEDUP_ATTACKS`, 13, 15, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, 30, 32 and 34. Every boss hit that lands from 17 to 28 is `reduced` and takes 40% of its roll, rounded down, off the boss; every other hit is `passed` and takes all of it.
- **Pixels.** The spotanim's translucent glow is found by its colour: blue at least 110, blue at least 35 above green and red at least 25 above green. `EXPLOSION_GLOW_BOX` runs from 128 px left to 127 px right of the canvas centre and from 16 px above to 79 px below it, under the 5x5's rim, whose purple also passes the colour test. Both glow shots are copied 85% into the tick (`EXPLOSION_GLOW_SHOT_FRACTION`). In `explosion-left.png` and `explosion-under.png` the glow is at least `EXPLOSION_GLOW_MIN_SHARE` (1.5%) of the box. In `explosion-left.png` its mean column lies at least 1% of the width from the centre, because the mark stays on the tile it captured while the camera follows the player; in `explosion-under.png`, where the player stays on the marked tile, it lies within 1.6%. The spotanim's shards are found by their colour: green at most 35, blue at least 100 and blue at least 40 above red, which the 5x5's rim, the boss and the floor fail. `EXPLOSION_OVERHEAD_BOX` runs from 100 px left to 99 px right of the canvas centre and from 150 px to 41 px above it, over the player's head. The overhead shots are copied 25% into the tick (`EXPLOSION_OVERHEAD_SHOT_FRACTION`). In `explosion-standing.png` the shards are at least `EXPLOSION_OVERHEAD_MIN_SHARE` (0.2%) of the box; in `explosion-before.png` and `explosion-after.png` they are at most `EXPLOSION_GLOW_MAX_SHARE` (0.1%).
- **Run end.** `drive_explosion_run_end` opens `?seed=38&practice=1` (`EXPLOSION_RUN_END_QUERY`), sets the boss's HP to 1 with `setBossHp(1)` after P4 tick 1, clicks the hovering boss on tick 15 (`EXPLOSION_KILL_CLICK_TICK`) with `click_boss`, steps two tiles on 16, and copies the canvas to `explosion-run-end-marked-1.png` and `-marked-2.png` 75% and 85% into 17 (`EXPLOSION_MARKED_SHOT_FRACTIONS`). The blowpipe's shot kills him at 18, before mark 17's impact. Then it copies the canvas 85% into each of the next two SDK world ticks, the probe's `tick`, because the P4 clock stops when the run ends. `log()` matches `EXPLOSION_RUN_END_EXPECTED`: the mark at 17 on (5, 1), `run-ended` kill at 18 and no `explosion-resolved`.
- **Pixels.** The run-end check counts the spotanim's glow and shard pixels and two footage colours, a bright purple glow (blue at least 120, blue at least 80 above green, red at least 50 above green) and the shards' `EXPLOSION_PIXEL` (`#5e317b`) within 12 per channel, in the glow box: at least 1.5% in the brighter marked copy and at most `EXPLOSION_RUN_END_MAX_SHARE` (0.2%) after the end, because the player's own purple weapon draws about 0.15% of the box. The mark's look is a one-shot spotanim that ends at its impact time by itself, so the after-end copies cannot tell the run-end removal apart from the look's own end. They catch a mark that would keep drawing.
- **Scripted.** `scripts/verify.sh browser` runs these steps in the `explosions`, `dodge_speedup` and `explosion_run_end` units and records `explosionsDodgedOnMarkTicks`, `lateStepHitOnMarkedTile`, `campingUnderBossHit`, `oneTileDodgesKeepTheBlowpipeFiring`, `hitsDuringTheExplosionsLoseTheReduction`, `explosionSoundsOnSchedule`, `markerStaysOnCapturedTile`, `markGlowsBrightPurple`, `shardsOverTheHeadOnlyOnMarkTicks` and `markGoneWhenTheRunEnds` in their units' `verdict.json`, with the measured shards under `observed.explosions.overhead`, the glow under `observed.explosions.glow`, the heard sounds under `observed.explosions.sounds` and the run-end page under `observed.explosions.runEnd`.

## Gotchas

- A screenshot can take most of a 600 ms tick, and longer on a software renderer. Copy the canvas inside the frame instead (`arm_shot` and `take_shot`, on the frame clock from `start_frame_clock`), and arm every shot of a page before taking the first, because encoding one copy on llvmpipe outlasted the rest of its tick.
- A dev server started with `scripts/verify.sh serve` can miss a file that `git checkout` replaces. After restoring a deliberate break, check the served bundle before trusting the next run. The `browser` stage starts its own server.
- A left-click on the floor under the hovering boss walks there. Click him at the height `boss_point` finds above (5, 5), which it reads two ticks before the click.
- Reacting to the mark is too late by design. The mark appears when the mark tick has already captured the tile, and a click after that moves the player on the impact tick. Click before the mark tick, as the rhythm of every 2 ticks allows.
- The camera follows the player with a short lag. Screenshots taken right after a dodge show the mark closer to the centre than it settles.
- `step_on_marks` reads the player's tile on the tick it clicks, so a step that falls short does not carry its error into the next one.
- A left-click under the boss attacks him once he is drawn. Walk under him while he is hidden (P4 ticks 0 to 3), or through the context menu.
- The `scripts/verify.sh browser` order runs this recipe before the loadout recipe, which opens its own page.
- Headless Chrome starts the audio context `running`, so this sound check does not test the resume on the first click. The [timing cues](./timing-cues.md) recipe does, in a session that starts each context suspended.
- The buffer lengths come from the SDK's own synthesizer run on cache 2746. A different cache or synthesizer can change them.
- The look is cache spotanim 4339 `vfx_void_chain_explosion`. Spotanims 4299 to 4302 are `vfx_ocellus_quadrant_bomb`, another attack, and 4340 `vfx_void_chain_player_telegraph` is the ring of dark ovals around the legs from the series start to the first mark, which the trainer does not play.
