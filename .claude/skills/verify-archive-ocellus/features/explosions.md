# Personal explosions

Five ticks after each lifeforce hit, Ocellus marks the tile the player starts that tick on, and marks again every 2 ticks, 6 times by default. Each mark plays the game's own effect, cache spotanim 4339, on the marked tile: on the mark tick long thin purple shards stand high around the player's head and close in on the tile, then a purple glow fills it, and a tick later grey dust and spikes burst out there. A player who starts that tick on the marked tile takes 16. The mark captures the tile before the player moves, so a player who moves during the mark tick leaves it behind, and a step first taken on the impact tick is too late. Standing under the boss gives no cover. Two sounds set the rhythm players step to: synth 12638 starts each series two ticks after the lifeforce hit, and synth 12537 plays on every mark.

## Sub-features

- `explosion-series-start` logs `explosion-series-started` at P4 tick 14 (inner void) and 45 (outer void), two ticks after each lifeforce hit, and plays 12638 on the player's tile.
- `explosion-mark` logs `explosion-marked` at P4 tick 17, 19, 21, 23, 25 and 27 (inner void) and 48 to 58 (outer void), with the tile the player started that tick on, and places the mark on that tile and plays 12537 on the player's tile.
- `explosion-impact` logs `explosion-resolved` a tick after each mark. The outcome is `hit` with a 16 receipt when the player starts the impact tick on the marked tile, `dodged` when the player starts it 1 tile away, and `avoided` when 2 or more tiles away. The mark bursts either way.
- `explosion-dodge` cuts the player's attack delay by 3, never below 0, on each `dodged` impact, and records the delay before and after in the outcome's `attackDelay`. A Scythe or a Twisted bow on Rapid then attacks every 2 ticks while each mark is dodged. A hit or a 2-tile run leaves the delay alone.
- `explosion-under-boss` records `underBoss: true` when the marked tile is under the boss. Such a mark still hits.
- `explosion-damage-reduction` cuts each player hit that lands on the boss while the explosions occur, from the first mark to the last impact (17 to 28 and 48 to 59), by 60% of the attacker's roll, rounded down, then caps it to the boss's HP as the SDK caps any hit, and logs it as `player-hit-judged` with outcome `reduced`. A Division glaive the reduction cuts to 0 drains nothing, so the second glaive rolls against the Defence the first one actually leaves. A later attack counts only glaives still in flight, so once both glaives land it rolls against the Defence they left. A hit the boss blocks still lands as 0. A drill whose focus leaves out the explosions has no reduction.
- `explosion-run-end` removes a mark still waiting for its impact when the run ends. A kill on an impact tick ends the run before that impact resolves, and the mark no longer draws under the result panel.
- `explosion-choices` select the series with `explosions`: `v2-tell-then-six` (default, marks from hit + 5, 17 to 27, in step with sound 12537), or `wiki-six` (marks from hit + 3, 15 to 25), and the reduction with `explosionDamageReduction`: `wiki-60` (default, the Strategies page) or `wiki-50` (the Ocellus page).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `http://127.0.0.1:8002/` and stand still after the lifeforce hit at P4 tick 12. The mark appears under the player at 17, glows halfway through the tick, and a 16 lands at 18.
- Click a neighbouring tile on each mark tick, or keep walking, and every mark is left behind.
- Click the boss with the Twisted bow, then on each mark tick click one tile away and click the boss again. The bow fires every 2 ticks from 18 to 28. A click 2 tiles away runs, so it avoids the mark without the speed-up. Each hit that lands from 17 to 28 takes 60% less off the boss bar.
- Listen for 12638 and 12537. **Sound effects** and **Area sounds** in the Settings menu start ticked until the player unticks one.
- Pick `Explosions` in the `Focus` selector and press `Restart` to practise the explosions alone.
- Open `?seed=38&bosshp=1&focus=explosions`, click the hovering boss after P4 tick 14 and click a tile beside you after 16. The mark on your old tile glows at 17, the bow's shot kills him at 18 before that mark goes off, and the mark disappears with the kill.
- Add `explosions=wiki-six` to the URL for the other reading of the footage.

## Driving it with agent-browser

Preconditions:

- The baseline preconditions in the index hold, the lifeforce-orb recipe has run, and the page shows its skipped-orb run with the seed field at 1 and the default ranged setup.
- A click must reach the page between two game ticks. `agent-browser batch` runs a `wait --fn` on the P4 tick and the `mouse move/down/up` that follow it in one call, which lands the click on the next tick. Separate CLI calls can miss it.

- **Sound on.** Before choosing the focus, turn on the SDK's sound and area sound the way a returning player's saved settings would: read the JSON in `localStorage` key `osrs-sdk:settings` (`{"version":1,"values":{...}}`), set `values.playsAudio` and `values.playsAreaAudio` to `true`, and write it back with `agent-browser eval`. The reload reads it. After P4 tick 0, wrap `AudioBufferSourceNode.prototype.start` with `agent-browser eval` so each start records the P4 tick, how far into that tick it came, the buffer's length and sample rate, and the audio context's state.
- **Dodge on mark ticks.** Run `agent-browser select "#focus" explosions`, then wait until `focus=explosions` is in the URL. Compute the canvas centre from `agent-browser get box "#world"`. In one batch, wait for P4 tick 16 with `>= 16` and click `DODGE_CLICK_SIDE_PX` (110) right of and `STEP_CLICK_DOWN_PX` (25) below the centre. That pixel is the tile two west of the player on its row, because the camera faces south. Save the canvas as `explosion-left.png` from inside the first frame 80% into tick 17, timed from the tick's start. It shows the purple glow on (5, 1) and the player on (3, 1). Repeat the batch click after ticks 18, 20, 22, 24 and 26, alternating left and right. `log()` shows a `walk-ordered` input in rows 17, 19, 21, 23, 25 and 27, the first mark on (5, 1), and `avoided` at 18, 20, 22, 24, 26 and 28.
- **Outside the series.** Screenshot `explosion-before.png` 5% into P4 tick 15, before the first mark, and `explosion-after.png` 5% into tick 29, after the inner series. Neither shows shards over the player's head.
- **Late step.** In one batch, wait for tick 48, click the tile two west, and screenshot `explosion-standing.png` 5% into the tick, timed from its start. The click lands on tick 49, the impact tick. ## Gotchas

- A screenshot can take most of a 600 ms tick. Time a look from the tick's start (`__tickStarts` for P4 ticks, `__worldTickStarts` for world ticks, both from `record_tick_starts`) instead of a fixed wait after the tick changes: frames 200 ms apart all landed in the first half of the tick in 3D, before the glow shows.
- A dev server started with `scripts/verify.sh serve` can miss a file that `git checkout` replaces. After restoring a deliberate break, check the served bundle before trusting the next run. The `browser` stage starts its own server.

- A left-click on the floor under the hovering boss walks there. Click his body, which the camera keeps above the player's column while the player steps south and back.
- Reacting to the mark is too late by design. The mark appears when the mark tick has already captured the tile, and a click after that moves the player on the impact tick. Click before the mark tick, as the rhythm of every 2 ticks allows.
- The camera follows the player with a short lag. Screenshots taken right after a dodge show the mark closer to the centre than it settles. 60% into the mark tick the glow sat about 19 px, under half a tile, left of the centre in the last run, while the player was drawn right of it.
- The dodges drift because the camera lags: runs stepped to (3, 1), (6, 1), (2, 1), (6, 1) and (2, 1). The north band runs from x 2 to 11, so the drift stays on floor.
- A left-click under the boss attacks him once he is drawn. Walk under him while he is hidden (P4 ticks 0 to 3), or through the context menu.
- Under `focus=explosions` the floor deals no damage, so camping under the boss in the inner void costs only the explosions. In the full fight the inner void floor would also hit.
- The `scripts/verify.sh browser` order runs this recipe before the loadout recipe, which opens its own page.
- Headless Chrome starts the audio context `running`, so this sound check does not test the resume on the first click. The [timing cues](./timing-cues.md) recipe does, in a session that starts each context suspended.
- The buffer lengths come from the SDK's own synthesizer run on cache 2746. A different cache or synthesizer can change them.
- The look is cache spotanim 4339 `vfx_void_chain_explosion`. Spotanims 4299 to 4302 are `vfx_ocellus_quadrant_bomb`, another attack, and 4340 `vfx_void_chain_player_telegraph` is the ring of dark ovals around the legs from the series start to the first mark, which the trainer does not play.
- The spotanim's shards stand over the head only in roughly the first quarter of the mark tick, and a screenshot can take most of a tick, so the overhead shots are timed 5% into the tick from its start.
