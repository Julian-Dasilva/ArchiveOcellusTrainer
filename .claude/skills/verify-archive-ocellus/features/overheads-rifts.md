# Overheads, heals and rifts

Ocellus wears a curse overhead, drawn under his HP bar with the cache's head icons: Deflect Melee from P4 tick 8 to 32, Soul Split from 33 to 37, none at 38, Deflect Range from 39 to 60, Soul Split again from 61 to 66 and none at 67, then the same each 60-tick cycle. A player hit of the deflected style lands as 0, and the player takes 10% of the damage it would have dealt, rounded down, as its own hitsplat. While the boss hovers over the inner void, from P4 tick 8 to 29, a melee player cannot reach him and does not swing. Ranged still lands. Two ticks after a transition attack hits an unprotected player, Soul Split heals the boss by the damage it dealt, and a heal splat shows on the boss. From cycle 2, on the tick before each inner void (P4 tick 67, then every 60 ticks), void rifts rise, 2 in cycle 2 and then 3, 4 and 5, each on its own seeded floor tile 2 to 5 tiles from the boss's 3x3. Under each rift's green HP bar a yellow timer bar of the same size fills from the left as the rift charges, and it is full on the burst tick, 30 ticks after spawn. The player can kill a rift, and every ranged or melee attack but a special attack hits it for its max hit. A rift dead by the end of tick 96 despawns, the chat shows "Destroying the void rift empowers you!", and the kill resets the player's attack delay, so the next attack can go at once. Every rift still standing at 97 detonates, and the player takes one hit of 28 to 41 under the void flare however many burst. The bar's fill direction is inferred from one frame of the owner's picture. The burst's tick and damage and the reset come from [p4-recorded-fights.md](./p4-recorded-fights.md).

## Sub-features

- `overhead-icon` draws sprite 440 frame 15 (Deflect Melee), 16 (Deflect Range) or 11 (Soul Split) under the boss's HP bar while the phase has that overhead. The lead-in has none, and neither has the tick before each void, S+30 and S+59.
- `deflect` logs `player-hit-judged` for each player hit on the boss with outcome `blocked`, `passed` or `invulnerable`, or `reduced` while the explosions occur ([explosions.md](./explosions.md)). A hit is judged by the overhead up on the tick it was launched, so a ranged hit launched before Deflect Range rises lands in full. A blocked hit lands as 0. Under `block-and-reflect` (default) the player takes `floor(requested / 10)` through the same damage path as every other hit, logged as `deflect-reflected` with the blocked hit's tick, style and receipt, and nothing when that is 0. `block-at-launch` blocks and reflects nothing, and `none` blocks nothing.
- `rift-max-hit` makes every ranged or melee attack but a special attack hit a rift for its max hit, with no accuracy or damage roll. The SDK still caps a hit at the rift's 44 HP. Each hit that lands on a rift logs `rift-hit` with its tick, rift, style and damage. The full fight's blowpipe hits a rift for 24 a dart, so it kills in two darts, 24 and the last 20. In the Vitest drill the Twisted Bow's max on a rift is 62 in the ranged setup and 53 in the melee setup, so it kills in one shot. Ralos throws two glaives of 18 (ranged setup) or 15 (melee setup) and needs two throws. The melee setup's scythe on Jab hits a rift once a swing for 35, its max, so the second swing lands the last 9. Magic and the Division special roll as on any target.
- `hover-melee-reach` keeps a melee attack from reaching the boss while he hovers. The inner-void phase carries boss state `present-out-of-melee-reach`, and a player with a melee weapon who targets him there stands in reach without swinging until he lands at 30. The `hoverMeleeReach` choice selects `unreachable` (default) or `reachable`, which gives the hover the landed state `present-damageable`.
- `soul-split-heal` logs `boss-healed` with cause `soul-split` and the attack's ordinal 2 ticks after an unprotected transition hit, by the damage that hit applied. A protected hit heals nothing. The heal splat stays for 2 ticks.
- `rift-spawn` logs `rift-spawned` for NPC 16678 rifts at 67 and every 60 ticks after: 2, 3, 4 and then 5 a cycle, each on its own seeded floor tile 2 to 5 from the boss's 3x3 and never on a hole. Seed 1's first two are `rift-67-0` on (4, 2) and `rift-67-1` on (2, 1). Each plays spawn sequence 14896 with its sound.
- `rift-timer` draws a 30 by 5 px bar directly under the rift's HP bar, `#1e1e0c` behind and `#e0c020` from the left over the share of the 30-tick charge done: empty at the spawn tick 67, 15/30 at 82 and full at 97.
- `rift-destroyed` logs each rift whose HP was 0 at the end of tick 96, at the next boss step, with the player's attack delay before and after the kill resets it to 0. The rift plays despawn sequence 14897, and the kill line goes to the chat.
- `rift-burst` logs one `rift-burst` at 97 naming every standing rift, with the one hit's receipt. Each rift plays detonate sequence 14898, and spotanim 4306 plays on the player.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `http://127.0.0.1:8002/` and watch the icon under the boss's HP bar change at P4 tick 33, 38, 39, 61 and 67. Attack the boss to see blocked hits as 0. With `?weapon=melee`, click the hovering boss and watch the player wait beside him until he lands.
- Wait for the rifts at P4 tick 67 and click one to attack it. The kill line shows in the chat.
- Let a transition attack hit without the matching prayer to see the Soul Split heal.

## Driving it with agent-browser

Preconditions:

- The baseline preconditions in the index hold, and the loadout recipe has run.
- The player stays on spawn (5, 1) until a step below clicks, so the camera matches the pixel boxes, which are relative to the canvas centre.
- A click timed to a P4 tick is `click_after_tick`, or a `tile_pixel` click on a named tile. Each dispatches `mousedown` and `mouseup` on `#world` in the first animation frame of that tick, and only when `document.elementFromPoint` is `#world`.
- Every page runs the whole cycle, so the floor, the explosions and the transition attacks hit the player too. The recipes use `practice=1`, so none of them ends a run.

- **Overhead icons.** Open `?seed=1&practice=1&weapon=blowpipe` (`RIFT_QUERY`) and wait for P4 tick 0, so the loading cover is gone. Save sound and area sound on and click Restart (`#restart_fight`), which reloads the same seed, then wait for P4 tick 0 and install the audio probe. Give no other input. Screenshot `overhead-5.png`, `overhead-31.png`, `overhead-35.png` and `overhead-45.png` at those P4 ticks (`OVERHEAD_SHOT_TICKS`). The script counts three icon colours in `ICON_BOX`, from 40 left to 40 right of the canvas centre and from 290 to 190 above it. The colours are the blue deflect arrows, the red of the Deflect Melee blade and the Soul Split arrows, and the orange Deflect Range fletching. Blue with red reads as Deflect Melee, blue with orange as Deflect Range, red alone as Soul Split, and neither as no icon. Each reading must equal the overhead that `log()` has at that tick and the next. The thresholds are 0.2% of the box for blue and red and 0.06% for orange.
- **No overhead before each void.** On the same page, install the overhead draw probe with the audio probe. It wraps `drawImage` on 2D canvas contexts and, on each animation frame, records the P4 tick from `snapshot()`, so each tick lists its frames and the `sprites/` images drawn into them. The boss's overhead is the only sprite the page draws. Save `window.__overheadDraws` after the burst. `OVERHEAD_GAP_DRAWS` holds: ticks 37, 38 and 39 draw `440-11`, nothing and `440-16`, and 66, 67 and 68 draw `440-11`, nothing and `440-15`. Each tick has at least one frame, and `log()` has overhead `null` at 38 and 67.
- **Kill a rift.** On the same page, on tick 67 read the `tileCentre` of `RIFT_SHOT` (`rift-67-1`)'s tile, on (2, 1), from its `rift-spawned` event. Screenshot `rift-spawned.png` at 68, then `click_after_tick` at 68 (`RIFT_CLICK_TICK`) clicks that pixel. The player keeps the default ranged setup and shoots it from spawn with the blowpipe. Wait for a `rift-destroyed` event, then wait 600 ms for the chat to poll. Screenshot `rift-destroyed.png` and run `agent-browser --json get text "#game_chat"`, whose last line is the kill line. Screenshot `rift-burst.png` at 97 (`RIFT_BURST_TICK`); it shows a 36 hitsplat on the player under the purple flare. Once `rift-67-1` is destroyed, read `tileCentre` of `RIFT_TIMED` (`rift-67-0`)'s tile into `rift-timed-tile.json`. `log()` matches `RIFT_EXPECTED`: `rift-spawned` at 67 for `rift-67-0` on (4, 2) and `rift-67-1` on (2, 1), `rift-67-1` destroyed by 97, and one `rift-burst` at 97 naming `["rift-67-0"]` with a requested 36. Its `rift-hit` events are `RIFT_RANGED_HITS`, `[[71,"rift-67-1","ranged",24],[73,"rift-67-1","ranged",20]]`.
- **Timer bar.** On the same page, screenshot `rift-charged.png` at 96 (`RIFT_CHARGED_SHOT_TICK`). The script counts the yellow `#e0c020` share of `RIFT_TIMER_BOX`, 10 px left to 18 px right of and 66 to 62 px above `rift-timed-tile.json`, which is `rift-67-0`'s timer bar. ## Gotchas

- In the inner void the risen boss's icon is above the top of the screen at the default camera. The icon shots wait for the landing at 30.
- osrs-sdk has no "I can't reach that!". The trainer's player declines a melee swing at the hovering boss the way the SDK declines any attack, by `attack()` returning false, so the player keeps the boss as its target, spends no attack delay and swings on the tick he lands. The tile it waits on is inner void, which hits the player there; Practice keeps the run going.
- osrs-sdk draws player overheads where the boss's HP banner covers them. The boss's icon sits under his HP bar, as the footage shows.
- A rift joins the world on the tick after the boss spawns it, because osrs-sdk adds new mobs at the next tick. The click waits for 68.
- The osrs-sdk scythe sends all three hits to a 1x1 target. The trainer's scythe hits a target once for each tile of its width, up to three, so a swing hits a rift once and the boss three times. The hits it skips never roll, and the one it keeps is the swing's first, full-damage hit, so a rift takes the scythe's full max.
- The page starts with the weapon `weapon` names, the twisted bow by default. The ranged recipes pin `weapon=blowpipe` and the melee ones `weapon=melee`.
- The deflect page wears the melee setup, so Deflect Range blocking shows on the reflect page and in Vitest.
- The footage shows a white wisp travelling to the boss before the Soul Split heal. No cache spotanim matches it, so the trainer does not draw it.
- osrs-sdk has no chatbox. The kill line goes to `#game_chat`, a page element over the canvas, and other lines stay above it, so read its last line.
- The `p4_cycle` page also logs rifts at 67. Its checks stop at 70 and do not read them.
- osrs-sdk plays a frame sound with a cache range only to a listener closer than that range, counting Manhattan tiles less one. The rift sounds have range 7. From spawn, (4, 2) and (2, 1) are 2 and 3 tiles away, so the player hears both. A rift 8 or more tiles away is silent.
- 12641 has the same length as the explosion series sound 12638, and the full cycle plays 12638 at 74, when the second series starts. The burst check looks for each burst sound among the starts of its length instead of requiring an exact list.
