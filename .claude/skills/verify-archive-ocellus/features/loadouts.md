# Loadouts

The player enters phase 4 with the weapon that Start with names in hand. The twisted bow (20997), the default, and the toxic blowpipe (12926) are worn with the ranged setup: the Masori mask, body and chaps (f), Zaryte vambraces and the Necklace of rupture. Melee wears Inquisitor's great helm, hauberk and plateskirt and the Amulet of rancour, with the Scythe of Vitur (22325) in hand. The blowpipe stays for the whole fight: whichever of the blowpipe and the setup's own weapon is not in hand takes the slot of the last Saradomin brew, inventory slot 26. Both wear Avernic treads (max), so a switch never changes the boots. Tonalztics of Ralos, the special attack weapon, sits in inventory slot 0, followed by every piece of the other setup that differs from the worn one. Clicking a switch equips it. Ralos's special attack, Division, spends half the special bar, throws two glaives and lowers the target's Defence by 12.5% of its Magic level for each glaive that deals damage. Against Ocellus (Magic 300) that is 37 Defence per damaging glaive.

## Sub-features

- `setup-worn` enters wearing the setup and weapon that the link's `weapon` or the `Start with` selector names, the twisted bow by default.
- `setup-switches` lists Ralos in slot 0 and the other setup's eight differing pieces in slots 1 to 8; clicking all eight wears the other setup, scythe included, from the ranged setup.
- `setup-weapon` carries the blowpipe in slot 26 when the setup's own weapon is in hand, and the bow when the blowpipe is.
- `set-effects` gives Inquisitor's armour its crush bonus on Jab. The ranged setup has no set effect. Vitest owns the attack-roll and max-hit literals, because the browser cannot force a max roll.
- `division` spends 50 special energy and, as the glaives land, drains the boss's Defence by 37 for each glaive that deals damage.
- `setup-selector` reloads the page on the same seed with the start chosen in the `Start with` selector when Restart is clicked.
- `tier-select` dresses the tier that the link's `tier` or the `Tier` selector names: `raid`, the default and the loadout above, or one of the five gear tiers `max`, `high`, `mid`, `low` and `poor` (`gear.tiers` in the data file). A tier holds only the items the sim can build with the wiki's bonuses, and the panel lists the rest under the selector as "Not simulated yet, so left out". `Start with` lists only the selected tier's starts. A `weapon` the tier does not offer starts with the tier's first start instead.
- `editor` opens from `Loadout` in the Fight section, before the start or by pausing a live run, and Resume closes it. It shows the tier, the worn slots, the 28-slot bag and Alliance Supplies as the shop, with "left" counts for the two stocked items the sim can build, Saradomin brew(4) and Super restore(4); the rest of the stock is listed as not simulated. A worn slot or bag slot offers only items whose bonuses the tier data checks against the wiki. Apply saves the tier's edit under `osrs-sdk:loadouts:archive-ocellus` and restarts on the same seed, and the run then wears and carries the edit, with `edited: true` in `log().header.loadout`. A saved edit that names an unknown item, wears an item in a slot it does not fit, holds a bag that is not 28 slots, or carries more of a supply than the tier plus the shop allows starts on the preset; `#loadout_note` says so under the button, and the editor names why with Reset. An edit of an older tier revision still starts the run, and the editor says "This tier changed since you edited it". When the browser refuses storage, the editor says edits won't be kept. `Start with` lists the tier's starts the active loadout can make, and "As worn" when it can make none.
- `defence-drained` opens phase 4 with his Defence at the 60% floor, 150, when `Defence drained` is ticked or the link has `drained=1`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `http://127.0.0.1:8002/`, `?weapon=blowpipe` or `?weapon=melee`.
- Open `?tier=low`, or pick a tier in the `Tier` selector above `Start with`, and press `Restart`.
- Pick a weapon in the `Start with` selector in the side panel's Fight section, or tick `Defence drained`, and press `Restart`.
- Click `Loadout` in the Fight section, edit the tier, and press `Apply`.
- Open the inventory tab (the backpack icon in the bottom row of the side panel) and click items to switch them.
- Click the special attack orb (the crossed swords beside the minimap) with Ralos wielded, then click Ocellus.

## Driving it with agent-browser

Preconditions:

- The baseline preconditions in the index hold, with the page opened at `http://127.0.0.1:8002/?seed=6&weapon=blowpipe&practice=1` (`LOADOUT_QUERY`), and P4 tick 9 reached (`snapshot().fight.run.tick >= 9`), so the boss is damageable.
- Panel points are given on a 1280x800 canvas. The canvas is narrower, because the side panel takes the right of the page, so add the canvas's left edge and `width - 1280` from `agent-browser --json get box "#world"` to each x, and its top edge, plus `height - 800` for a point below y 400, to each y. `click_panel` in `scripts/verify.sh` does this. The constants sit together under `LOADOUT_` at the top of `scripts/verify.sh`.

- **Worn setup.** Run `agent-browser --json eval "window.__archiveOcellusProbe.snapshot().fight.player"`. `equipped` is `LOADOUT_RANGED_WORN`, `{"weapon":12926,"offhand":null,"helmet":27235,"chest":27238,"legs":27241,"gloves":26235,"cape":28951,"necklace":33639,"feet":31097,"ring":28310,"ammo":11212}`, and `inventory` starts with `28922, 22325, 24419, 24420, 24421, 22981, 21295, 29801, 25485` and holds 20997 in slot 26.
- **Inventory tab.** Click the panel point (1164, 745). Ralos and the Inquisitor's pieces show icons drawn from cache 2746, as do the Masori (f) pieces and Zaryte vambraces once they are switched into the inventory. The other items show the SDK's bundled icons. Screenshot it.
- **Worn icons.** Click the equipment tab at the panel point (1197, 745) and screenshot it. It shows the Masori (f) pieces and Zaryte vambraces with their cache icons. Click the inventory tab again before the switches.
- **Switches.** Slot `n` is the panel point (1125 + 39 × (n mod 4), 508 + 32 × floor(n / 4)). Click slots 1 to 8 the same way, about 150 ms apart. `equipped` becomes `LOADOUT_MELEE_WORN`, `{"weapon":22325,"offhand":null,"helmet":24419,"chest":24420,"legs":24421,"gloves":22981,"cape":21295,"necklace":29801,"feet":31097,"ring":25485,"ammo":11212}`, and the player model shows the scythe and the dark Inquisitor plate.
- **Division.** Click slot 0 to wield Ralos and click the special orb at the panel point (1140, 157) so it lights up. Then click Ocellus with `click_boss`, which finds the height above (5, 5) where the right-click menu offers Attack first. Wait with `agent-browser wait --fn "window.__archiveOcellusProbe.snapshot().fight.player.specialEnergy < 100"`, then wait until `log().rows` holds two `player-hit-judged` events, because each glaive drains only as it lands. `specialEnergy` is 50, and `fight.boss.defence` has dropped from 250 by `LOADOUT_DIVISION_DRAIN` (37) for each glaive that kept damage. A glaive that lands during the explosions keeps 40% of its damage, rounded down, so a glaive of 1 keeps 0 and drains nothing. The verdict reads the two glaives from `log()` and counts one drain for each glaive whose damage after the reduction is above 0. Two splats show on the boss and the orb reads 50.
- **Selector.** Run `agent-browser select "#start_weapon" melee` and `agent-browser click "#restart_fight"`. The page reloads with `weapon=melee` on the same seed. `equipped` is `LOADOUT_MELEE_WORN`, with the scythe, 22325, as the weapon, and `inventory` holds the blowpipe, 12926.
- **Defaults and Defence drained.** Open `?seed=6&practice=1&drained=1` (`START_OPTIONS_QUERY`) and wait for P4 tick 1. The weapon is the twisted bow, 20997 (`START_DEFAULT_WEAPON`), `inventory` holds the blowpipe, and `fight.boss.defence` is 150 (`START_DRAINED_DEFENCE`).
- **Tier.** Open `?seed=6&practice=1&tier=low&weapon=twistedBow` (`TIER_QUERY`) and wait for P4 tick 1. The low tier carries no twisted bow, so the blowpipe, 12926, is in hand. `equipped` is `TIER_LOW_WORN`, `{"weapon":12926,"offhand":null,"helmet":null,"chest":null,"legs":null,"gloves":7462,"cape":21295,"necklace":null,"feet":null,"ring":null,"ammo":11212}`; the snapshot reads each item's drawn id, and the SDK draws Seeking dragon arrows (33595) as Dragon arrow 11212, while `log().header.loadout` records 33595. `#start_tier` reads `low`, `#start_weapon` offers only `blowpipe`, and `#tier_not_simulated` starts with "Not simulated yet, so left out: Emberlight".
- **Editor.** Open `?seed=6&practice=1&tier=max&weapon=ralos` (`EDITOR_QUERY`) and wait for P4 tick 1. Click `#loadout`. `#resume` shows and `#loadout_editor` opens. Click `#loadout_editor [aria-label='Take Saradomin Brew']`. The preset select reads "Max (edited)" and the shop reads "Saradomin Brew5 left". Run `agent-browser find role button click --name Apply`. ## Gotchas

- The prayer tab is open on load. Open the inventory tab before you click a slot, or the click toggles a prayer.
- Special energy regenerates 10 every 30 seconds. Read it as soon as the glaives land.
- Without a pinned `seed`, the glaive rolls differ between loads, and a load where both glaives deal 0 leaves Defence at 250.
- A click on Ocellus before P4 tick 4 finds no target, because the boss is hidden.
- The boss's model hovers above the tile under him, so a click on (5, 5)'s floor pixel can miss him. Find his height in the frame that clicks, as `click_boss` in `scripts/verify.sh` does.
- Restart applies every choice in the side panel. Click `Restart` only after the `Start with` selector shows the weapon you want.
