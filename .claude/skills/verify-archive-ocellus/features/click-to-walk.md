# Click to walk

Left-clicking a floor tile outlines that tile, and the player runs to it at two tiles per game tick. Holding Control while clicking walks the player there at one tile per tick instead, as in game, and the run orb stays on. A click past the platform's edge, or on the raised brazier walkway, walks the player to the floor tile nearest the click, and the player stays there. In the outer void, the melee phase, the boss's 3x3 blocks the player, who runs or walks around his edge instead of under him; in every other stage the player can run under him.

## Sub-features

- `walk-click` sets the destination to the clicked floor tile.
- `walk-arrive` moves the player to the destination over the following ticks.
- `walk-camera` keeps the camera centred on the player while it moves.
- `walk-edge` stops the player on the platform's edge when the click lands off the platform.
- `walk-walkway` stops the player on the floor beside the walkway when the click lands on the walkway.
- `walk-correct` lets a later click replace an earlier one until the tick: the last click before a world tick decides where the player goes, however late in the tick it comes.
- `walk-control` walks one tile per tick on a Control-click. On a Mac the browser treats Control-click as a context click, and the trainer opens no menu; a real right-click still opens it.
- `walk-around-boss` runs around the boss's 3x3 in the outer void and straight under him in the other stages.
- `walk-corner` reaches (3, 8) and (4, 9) in the south pocket beside the 5x5's corner, where the old rows had a hole and left (4, 10) jutting out.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Left-click any floor tile in the game view.
- Left-click the black beyond the platform's edge.
- Hold Control and left-click a floor tile.

## Gotchas

- The camera faces south, so screen left is east and screen up is south.
- `agent-browser click "#world"` clicks the canvas centre, which is the tile just south of the player. Use `mouse` at the tile's projection.
- The projection is the floor tile's centre. Where the boss or another clickable is drawn over that point, the click selects it instead, as a hand-placed click would; `projectedClicksOrderTheirTiles` then fails, because the click orders no walk.
- A click on a tile right beside the edge does not walk onto it. That tile holds a movement blocker, so the SDK orders a nearby open tile and the player walks to the floor tile nearest that order.
- Wait for tick 8 before clicking so the proof starts after the GET READY countdown.
- `agent-browser mouse` takes no modifier keys. `control_click_at` in `scripts/verify.sh` sends the Control-click through CDP's `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` with the Ctrl modifier (2), on the page whose URL starts with the server's.
- On macOS, Chrome reports a Control-click as a primary `mousedown` with `ctrlKey` and then fires `contextmenu`; the page counts those `contextmenu` events, and `controlClickOpensNoMenu` needs at least one on a Mac.
- The world ticks every 600 ms. Wait for the arrival condition, not a fixed sleep.
- In `agent-browser batch`, a click lands 30 to 110 ms after the moment its `wait` returned on the laptop and 108 to 170 ms on CI's llvmpipe, so a CDP click aimed 500 ms into a tick often lands after the boundary. `drive_click_correction` dispatches its two clicks in the page instead and records where each mousedown landed.
- Click each corner tile from spawn on a fresh page. A second click from (3, 8) lands on a different tile depending on how far the trailing camera has caught up.
- From (7, 5), a click 200 px right of the centre, or 40 px below that, can land on the landed boss's model and attack him instead of walking. Click 90 px below it, where the floor beyond him is clear.
- A click on the tile under the landed boss's centre lands on his model and targets him instead of walking. A click on his north edge row reaches the floor.
- The tick-38 click must land before the world steps to 39. `click_after_tick` waits and clicks in one `agent-browser batch`; a late click records its order on row 40 and fails the check rather than passing it.
- The rendered model trails the logical tile by up to one tick. Wait about one second after arrival before the after screenshot.
